## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of high-tier distributed systems engineering and senior executive fiscal stewardship. Tailored for an elite Senior Software Engineer & Tech Lead with 15+ years bridging mission-critical code with corporate enterprise finance, the aesthetic merges the razor-sharp precision of developer tooling environments (Linear, Vercel) with the authoritative typographic discipline of Swiss architectural publishing and financial whitepapers (Stripe Press).

The emotional tone balances high-frequency engineering execution with institutional gravitas: calculated, calm, unshakeable, and highly lucrative. Visual treatments are austere and dense yet impeccably readable. Surfaces are built from deep, obsidian-tinted dark slates rather than hollow absolute blacks, paired with crisp micro-borders and deliberate accents in electric hyper-precision neon lime and mint. Financial impacts, key operational metrics (e.g., millions saved, sub-millisecond latencies, billions under control), and architecture schematics are staged with museum-grade typographic discipline.

## Colors

The palette operates in a calibrated deep dark-slate color spectrum, rejecting pure monochrome black in favor of blue-slate foundations that reduce glare and elevate chromatic contrast.

- **Primary (`#BAF336`):** Precision Neon Lime. Used with surgical discretion for critical state indicators, high-yield ROI highlights, interactive micro-cues, and key metric calls. It commands instant visual priority without overwhelming the workspace.
- **Secondary (`#34D399`):** Emerald Mint. Represents stability, operational resilience, and healthy system status. Used in production telemetry cards, SLA uptimes, and positive fiscal differentials.
- **Tertiary (`#93C5FD`):** Architectural Slate Blue. Applied to infrastructure badges, pipeline stages, distributed cloud nodes, and technical taxonomy tags.
- **Neutral (`#0A0D12`):** Core Obsidian Ground. Anchors canvas surfaces. Tiered upward to `#11151C` (cards, containers) and `#181E27` (elevated drawers, popovers, code headers).
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text is `#F4F1EA` (warm editorial off-white), preventing eye fatigue during intense code and data reviews. Subordinate content degrades progressively into `#94A3B8` and `#56657A`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes instant executive competence through a tri-font matrix:

1. **Structural Geometric Core (Manrope):** Handles dominant headlines, executive titles, core body copy, and big numeric callouts. Its modernist proportions provide structural clarity and humanized geometric warmth.
2. **Technical Instrumentation (JetBrains Mono):** Drives metadata tags, commit indicators, protocol paths, pipeline step indexes, and technical micro-labels. Kerning is locked to monospaced rhythmic tables (`0.06em` letter spacing on caps).
3. **Editorial Financial Nuance (Playfair Display):** Introduced selectively in italic/medium formats for high-level quotes, philosophical architectural framing, and strategic institutional context (e.g., "Engineering that connects distributed systems, product and financial return").

Text scale transitions smoothly from monumental desktop presentations down to dense mobile data tables without clipping or awkward line wraps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an authoritative 12-column mathematical grid with strict vertical 8pt baselines. 

- **Structure:** Maximum content width caps at 1320px for ultrawide consistency, centering within high-contrast technical margin gutters.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major modules (Hero, Metrics Matrix, Interactive Architecture, Case Studies) breathe with expansive vertical clearances (`space-3xl` on desktop, transitioning to `space-2xl` on mobile), generating an uncluttered, museum-grade portfolio presence.
- **Density:** Micro-components (data grids, metrics clusters, technology stacks) utilize tight, disciplined internal padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`), balancing breathing room with information density appreciated by engineering leaders and recruiters.
- **Adaptive Breakpoints:**
  - *Mobile (<768px):* 4-column reflow, stacked metric displays, full-width border-separated architecture tiers.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1024px):* 8-column layout, dual-metric card rows, condensed sidebar layouts.
  - *Desktop (>1024px):* Full 12-column asynchronous multi-tier architecture canvases and side-by-side technical deep dives.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of precise luminescent tiering, frosted glass refraction, and razor micro-borders:

- **Surface Layering:**
  - *Tier 0 (Base Canvas):* `#0A0D12` background, punctuated by a faint 1px micro-dot structural grid pattern (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`).
  - *Tier 1 (Cards & Data Panels):* `#11151C` with a 1px continuous border of `#1E2633`.
  - *Tier 2 (Modals, Code Terminals, Hover Focus):* `#181E27` with crisp boundary borders (`#2D384B`) and localized micro-glows.
- **Gradients & Radial Backdrops:** Subtle, diffused radial gradients (`rgba(186, 243, 54, 0.04)` and `rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.03)`) sit behind flagship architecture diagrams to create optical atmospheric depth without cluttering readability.
- **Glassmorphism:** Navigation rails and persistent status bars use background blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with semi-transparent tinted slate (`rgba(10, 13, 18, 0.82)`) and bottom hair-line dividers (`rgba(30, 38, 51, 0.9)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a disciplined "Soft" curvature philosophy (`roundedness: 1`). Radii are strictly bounded:

- **Core Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Micro-chips):** 4px (`0.25rem`) corner rounding. This preserves an engineered, industrial aesthetic reminiscent of modern CAD software and IDEs.
- **Cards & Data Modules:** 8px (`0.5rem`) corner rounding, balancing structure with modern ergonomics.
- **Pill Badges (System Status, Live Availability):** Strictly reserved for online/status indicators (9999px) where physical semantic metaphor demands a pill shape.
- **Interactive State Borders:** Border widths remain fixed at 1px hairline precision to emphasize architectural calculation.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Executive Action / Contact):** Surface `#BAF336`, text `#0A0D12`, font Manrope 600. On hover, background shifts to slightly desaturated chartreuse `#C8F75A` with subtle box-shadow `0 0 16px rgba(186, 243, 54, 0.3)`. Active transforms scale to `0.98`.
- **Secondary (Case Study Deep Dive):** Surface `transparent`, 1px border `#1E2633`, text `#F4F1EA`. On hover, border transitions to `#94A3B8` and background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Ghost / Code Copy:** JetBrains Mono font, 11px uppercase label, transparent surface with soft icon container; provides instantaneous visual confirmation tooltip upon copy.

### Executive KPI Metric Cards
- Designed to showcase financial impact (e.g., "R$ 24M/ano salvos", "100M msgs/dia", "R$ 100 bi em ativos sob custódia").
- Features a two-part construction: a top mono header (`label-mono`) with status pulsing dot, followed by a monumental statistic (`metric-stat`), and an annotated subtext detailing technical scope and business ROI.
- Subtle inner border highlights (`1px solid #1E2633`) paired with a discrete top corner hover glow in `#BAF336`.

### Architecture Diagrams & Interactive System Flow
- Clean dark-slate node containers connected via 1px vectors.
- Active nodes render with mint/lime pulsing status rings. Inactive or legacy layers render in muted `#56657A`.
- Interactive tooltip drawer slides in from the right edge with code snippets, latency benchmarks, and architectural decisions (Hexagonal, Event-Driven, DDD).

### Chips & Technology Taxonomy Badges
- Constructed with JetBrains Mono, 11px uppercase, padding `4px 8px`.
- Inactive: Background `#11151C`, border `#1E2633`, text `#94A3B8`.
- Active/Highlight: Background `rgba(186, 243, 54, 0.08)`, border `rgba(186, 243, 54, 0.4)`, text `#BAF336`.

### Project Showcase & Tech Recruiter Drawer
- Project cards feature split layouts: system metric breakdown on the left, interactive architecture or stack breakdown on the right.
- Fast metadata scanning bar for recruiters: Role, Team Size, Scale, Core Tech, and Direct Fiscal Impact.
- Expandable drawer containing structured bullet points formatted around: *Problem*, *Scale & Complexity*, *Architectural Decision*, and *Measured Outcome*.

### Inputs & Terminal Fields
- Minimalist input containers with `#0A0D12` background, 1px border `#1E2633`, placeholder `#56657A`. Focus ring transitions crisp hairline border to `#BAF336` without thick outlines.
